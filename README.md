# Café Calle Ocho – Ordering Prototype

**Course:** EGN-475  
**Student:** Nabin Chaulagain  
**Option chosen:** A – Café Calle Ocho (Little Havana)

## Problem statement

Café Calle Ocho currently takes every order at the counter, where one employee writes it on a ticket, takes payment, and shouts it back to the kitchen. This causes long lines during the morning rush and mixed-up or forgotten orders for regular customers who are in a hurry, and it causes stress and lost tickets for the kitchen staff who have no clear list of what is in progress. Our prototype will let customers browse the menu and place their order from a web page, and give the kitchen a live board where each order moves from received to preparing to ready to picked up, so that customers spend less time waiting in line and the staff always know exactly what to make next and what is done.

## Personas

**Marta Rodríguez (customer)** – 38, receptionist, regular every weekday morning. Wants to order ahead and grab her café con leche without standing in line. Medium tech comfort, hates being forced to create accounts.  
> "I love this place but I don't have ten minutes to stand in line. Just let me order on my phone and grab it."

**Luis Fernández (kitchen staff)** – 52, head of the kitchen for 9 years. Wants to see all open orders in one place and tap when something is done. Low-to-medium tech comfort, wants big buttons and no typing during the rush.  
> "Don't give me something complicated. Show me what to cook, let me tap when it's done, that's it."

## Must-have features

1. Menu page – items with name, description, price, grouped by category
2. Place an order – pick items and quantities, enter a pickup name, get an order number
3. Kitchen board – open orders as cards, buttons to move received → preparing → ready → picked up
4. Order status page – customer checks their order number and sees the stage
5. Order history – past orders with time, items and final status
6. Menu admin – add, edit, hide or delete items and change prices
7. Mark item sold out – one tap hides an item from the menu

## Not in this prototype

- Online payments (pay at the counter on pickup)
- SMS / email notifications
- Customer accounts or login
- Native mobile app (it is a mobile-friendly website)
- Loyalty points or coupons
- Delivery
